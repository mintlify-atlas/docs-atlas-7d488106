NVIDIA GPUs can work with Hyprland, but require a few extra configuration steps compared to AMD or Intel hardware. For maximum performance, the proprietary NVIDIA drivers with open-source kernel modules are recommended for modern GPUs. Because the official drivers are still proprietary, some issues may arise that the Hyprland team has no direct control over — however, driver quality has been steadily improving, and a smooth, near-parity experience with other GPU vendors is achievable.
